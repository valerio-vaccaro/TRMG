## Generate a mnemonic with a D8 die

Use an eight-sided die to generate 12 or 24 words. Each word requires 11 bits of entropy.

Roll the die until you have 11 bits, using this conversion table:

|Result|Bits|
|------|----|
|1|000|
|2|001|
|3|010|
|4|011|
|5|100|
|6|101|
|7|110|
|8|111|

If the final roll provides more bits than needed, retain only the leftmost bits required to reach 11 bits.

Use the [binary words table](binary-table.md) to match the resulting 11-bit value to a BIP-39 word.
